## Why You Can Trust It

Most AI chatbots sometimes **invent** verses and hadith that don't exist.
Noor AI is built so that can't happen:

- It **searches the actual sources** before answering — and you watch it happen
- Every citation — like **[Quran 2:255]** or **[Sahih al-Bukhari 8]** — is pulled
  **from the source text itself**, never written by the AI
- You can look up any reference and **verify it yourself**
- If the sources don't cover your question, it **says so** — no made-up answers

> An answer you can't verify isn't knowledge. Every answer here is checkable.

## Respects How You Follow the Faith

Choose your **school of thought** — Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, or Hanbali —
and answers on matters of practice are framed accordingly:

- Leads with **your school's position**
- **Names where scholars differ** — honestly, without pretending consensus
- For sensitive personal matters, it advises consulting **a qualified scholar**

> Careful where it should be careful. That's by design.
