# Noor AI — User Demo Video Script

Paired with `demo.md` (9 slides). Target runtime: **3–4 minutes** at a relaxed
pace (~140 wpm). Total narration ≈ 500 words.

This is the **user-facing demo** — no architecture, no code, no AWS. It shows
what the app does and why it can be trusted. Suitable for a resume link, a
LinkedIn post, or the top of the repo README.

## Production notes

- Record the **demo footage first** — it's the heart of the video (~2 of the
  3–4 minutes). Adjust narration to what actually happens on screen.
- Structure: slides 1–4 set up the promise (~1 min) → long demo cutaway
  (~2 min, with slides 5–8 available as section cards if you want breathers) →
  closing slide (~15 s).
- Speak like you're showing a friend an app you made, not pitching investors.
- Keep the cursor deliberate during the demo — slow movements, brief pauses
  on citations so viewers can read them.

## PART 1 — The Promise (~1 min, slides 1–4)

### Slide 1 — Title

> This is Noor AI — "Noor" means *light* in Arabic. It's a free web app where
> you can ask any question about Islam, in Arabic or English, and get an
> answer drawn directly from the Quran and the two most trusted hadith
> collections. Let me show you what makes it different.

### Slide 2 — What is Noor AI?

> Ask in your own words — no special phrasing needed. The answers come from
> three sources: the complete Quran, all of Sahih al-Bukhari, and all of
> Sahih Muslim. It's a conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions
> naturally. And there's nothing to install — it runs in your browser.

### Slide 3 — Why You Can Trust It

> Here's the part I care most about. If you've used AI chatbots for religious
> questions, you may have noticed they sometimes quote verses or hadith that
> simply don't exist. Noor AI is built differently: it searches the actual
> source texts before it answers, and every citation you see is pulled from
> the sources themselves — the AI never writes a reference on its own.
>
> That means you can take any citation it gives you — chapter and verse, or
> hadith number — look it up, and verify it yourself. And when the sources
> don't cover a question, it tells you that honestly instead of making
> something up.

### Slide 4 — Demo divider

> Enough talking — let me show you.

## PART 2 — 🎬 DEMO (~2 min of screen recording)

Narrate over the recording. Suggested beats — adapt to what actually happens:

### Beat 1 — First question, watch it search (~40 s)

> This is the live app. I'll ask: *"What are the five pillars of Islam?"*
>
> Watch what happens before the answer — you can see it working: searching
> the Quran… now the hadith collections. It found its sources. Now the
> answer streams in.
>
> Look at these references — Sahih al-Bukhari 8, Quran 2:255. These aren't
> generated by the AI — they're the actual source references, and you can
> look any of them up yourself.

### Beat 2 — Follow-up, it remembers (~20 s)

> It's a real conversation. I'll just say: *"tell me more about the third
> one"* — and it knows I mean zakat, from the answer it just gave. No need
> to repeat myself.

### Beat 3 — Arabic (~25 s)

> It's fully bilingual. I'll ask in Arabic: *«ما هي آية الكرسي؟»* — "What is
> Ayat al-Kursi?" The answer comes back in Arabic, with the same verified
> citations. Same sources, same rigor, your language.

### Beat 4 — School of thought (~25 s)

> One more thing. Islamic practice differs between schools of thought — so
> you can pick yours. I'll switch to Hanafi and ask about witr prayer.
> Notice the answer leads with the Hanafi position, and then honestly names
> where the other schools differ. It never pretends there's agreement where
> there isn't.

### Beat 5 — Feedback (~10 s)

> And if an answer helps you — or doesn't — tap thumbs up or down. That
> feedback comes straight to me and directly shapes the next version.

*(Slides 5–8 mirror these beats — use them as section cards between demo
segments if you want visual breathers, or skip them and run the demo as one
continuous take.)*
